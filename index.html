Bio

Great White Noise is a rock band based in Peterborough, ON, Canada that exclusively covers Canadian artists. There are so many great Canadian musicians and our setlist samples a wide cross section of our favorites (though Tragically Hip are hugely over-represented!).


The bandmembers are:

Mike - Drums
Chris - Bass, backing vocals
Lucas - Lead guitar, vocals
Joe - Lead vocals, guitar, keyboard, harmonica, fiddle
